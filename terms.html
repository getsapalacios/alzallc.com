Cancellation

Either party may cancel the service for any reason by giving the other party at least 30 days' written notice. Email to the address on file counts as written notice.

The cancellation takes effect 30 days after the notice is received (the "end date"). Until the end date, Alza keeps all services running and the client pays the monthly fee as usual. The fee for the final partial month is prorated to the end date.

Within five business days after the end date, Alza turns off the automations it manages, removes its access to the client's accounts, and gives the client a copy of the client's own data and settings it holds.

Either party may end the service sooner, on written notice, if the other party materially breaches these terms or the service agreement and does not fix the breach within 10 days, or as the payment terms allow for nonpayment.
