Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Alza Services LLC ("Alza," "we," "us") is a Texas company that builds AI-assisted automations for small businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version

  • Our website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.
  • We collect information only when you book a call with us.
  • We use it to prepare for and follow up on that call.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Information we collect

When you visit alzallc.com. Our website is a small static site of two pages, a home page and this policy. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising code. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs for security and operations. We do not receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

When you book a call. Our booking link takes you to Cal.com, a scheduling service. The booking form asks for:

  • your name and email address
  • your business type
  • your yearly revenue range
  • the task that takes the most time each week
  • when you want it fixed

When you email us or meet with us. We keep the messages you send and the notes we take during the call.

We do not ask for and do not want sensitive information such as health details, government ID numbers or payment card numbers. Please do not include them in the booking form or in email.

How we use your information

We use it to:

  • confirm and run your call
  • understand your business before we meet, so the call is useful
  • follow up about the services you asked about
  • keep a simple record of our conversations with you

We do not use it for anything unrelated to these purposes, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

AI-assisted processing

We are a small team, and we use AI assistant tools to help us work. These tools may help us read and summarize your booking details and emails, draft replies, and keep our records organized. A person at Alza reviews and sends every message to you. Your booking data is not used to train AI models, and we do not sell it to AI providers.

Where your information is stored

  • Cal.com holds your booking and sends the confirmation.
  • Google (Gmail and Google Meet) delivers our email and hosts the video call.
  • Our customer records are kept in a private database on a company computer, not in a shared online system.
  • AI assistant providers process information when we use their tools, as described above.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We share only what is needed for the service it provides.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We disclose it only to the service providers listed above, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep your booking information for 1 year from our last contact with you, and then we delete it. You may ask us to delete it sooner, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • give you a copy of it

Email alza.unit@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy request." We will respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, we will explain why, and you may appeal by replying to that message. Texas residents have these rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Email from us

If we send you marketing email in the future, every message will include a way to unsubscribe, and we will honor your request within 10 business days.

Security

We protect your information with access controls, account two-factor authentication, and restricted file permissions on the computer that stores our records. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit who and what can reach your data.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell people we are actively working with.
